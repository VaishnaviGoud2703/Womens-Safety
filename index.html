🛡️ Official Step-by-Step Reporting & Account Hardening Guides

📸 Instagram Impersonation & Harassment

1. Tap '...' on offender's profile → Report → Report Account.
2. Select It's pretending to be someone else → choose Me or Someone I know.
3. For photo misuse: Visit Instagram's Help Center to submit a government ID verification form.

💬 WhatsApp Harassment & Unknown Numbers

1. Open chat → tap contact info → Report Contact → check Block contact and delete chat.
2. Enable Two-Step Verification: Settings → Account → Two-Step Verification.
3. Protect IP in calls: Settings → Privacy → Advanced → Protect IP address in calls.

🌐 Official Cybercrime Reporting

India: Call 1930 (Financial cyber fraud) or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
US: File complaint at ic3.gov (FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center).
UK: Report to Action Fraud UK.
